PARTICIPATION CREDITS · COMPETITION WIN RATE · ENGAGEMENT BADGES

My Activity & Achievements

Track your progression from Saved (0 credits) → Registered (5 credits) → Attended (20 total credits) → Won a Hackathon/Competition (50 total credits). Credits are non-cumulative per event.

Non-Cumulative Event Credit Rules

Saved: 0 credits · Registered: 5 credits · Attended: 20 total credits · Won Competition/Hackathon: 50 total credits. Updating an event from Registered to Attended sets its contribution to 20 (not 25). Repeated updates never duplicate credits.

Competition Win-Rate Formula & Verification

Win Rate = Wins ÷ Completed Competitions (Attended + Won/Not Won outcome) × 100. Workshops, conferences, webinars, registrations, and pending outcomes are excluded. User entries are labelled Self-Reported until marked Admin Verified by faculty.
